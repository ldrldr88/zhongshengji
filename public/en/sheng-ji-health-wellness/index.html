Separate traditional language from medical facts

Longevity, protection and vitality are cultural terms

They describe belief, intention or symbolic interpretation. They do not establish longer life, lower disease risk, changed test results or fertility outcomes.

This service is not healthcare

Symptoms, chronic conditions, abnormal tests, mental distress, fertility and sleep concerns require qualified professional assessment. Do not stop medication, delay follow-up or replace diagnosis, treatment or prevention with a ceremony.

A client report is not medical evidence

Mood, sleep, appearance and test results can change for many reasons. A personal account does not prove causation or predict another person’s result.

What to do when health is a concern

Address medical needs first

For urgent symptoms, ongoing problems or abnormal test results, contact a doctor or local medical service first.

Reject fear-based selling

Pause if anyone says a disaster is certain or immediate payment is necessary to prevent harm. Divination or ritual language cannot replace risk assessment.

Check biological-sample safety

This site does not provide self-collection instructions. Ask about a non-invasive alternative and confirm medical, courier, customs, privacy, storage and disposal requirements.

Verify the service before paying

Confirm deliverables

Confirm the practitioner, site, date, RMB fees, process record, personal-material handling, return items and after-service contact in writing.

Separate evidence from interpretation

Identity, site, price and process may be verified. Benefit claims, cases and metaphysical interpretations may not be independently verifiable.

Keep an exit option

Obtain rescheduling, refund and cancellation terms before paying. Pause if safety, privacy or advertising claims remain unclear.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Sheng Ji treat disease or extend life?

This site has no evidence that it diagnoses, treats or prevents disease, extends life or changes medical test results. Seek qualified professional care.

Can I stop medication or reduce treatment?

Do not stop medication, change treatment or delay testing because of a traditional cultural service. Follow your clinician’s advice.

Do health testimonials prove an effect?

They can describe a personal experience, but they do not establish medical causation or predict another person’s outcome.
