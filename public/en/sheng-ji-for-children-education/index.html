Education outcomes depend on practical preparation

No score, admission or school result is guaranteed

Sheng Ji is a traditional cultural service. It does not establish improved grades, steadier exam performance, changed admissions decisions or helpful connections.

Prioritise evidence-based support

Teaching, practice, sleep, mental-health support, language skills, application materials and qualified admissions advice have observable roles. A cultural-service purchase should not displace essential education or living costs.

Reject urgency based on parental anxiety

Pause if anyone says a child will lose a once-only window or suffer lifelong consequences without immediate payment. There is no verified starting age or fixed benefit period.

Minors need additional protection

Protect the child’s wishes and privacy

Explain the service and cost and minimise collection of names, birth data, images, school details and grades.

Do not instruct a minor to collect a biological sample

This site does not provide blood self-collection instructions. Ask for a non-invasive alternative and confirm safety, transport, privacy and disposal requirements.

Do not act secretly for another person

Do not collect or send a child’s or adult family member’s personal items or data without informed consent.

Family checklist before deciding

Confirm the complete RMB cost

Published prices are RMB 72,000 for five years and RMB 500,000 for lifetime, with a RMB 10,000 booking deposit. Consider the opportunity cost and obtain a written quotation.

Confirm the deliverable process

Verify the practitioner, site, date, materials, records, return items, refund or rescheduling terms and after-service contact.

Turn wishes into practical actions

Translate abstract goals into study plans, application milestones, psychological support and family communication, then review actual progress.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Sheng Ji guarantee exam or admission results?

No. This site does not guarantee scores, admission, school placement, overseas-study applications or future career results.

What information should a minor provide?

Minimise personal data and obtain a written list before paying. This site does not instruct minors to collect blood or other biological samples.

What should parents do first?

Prioritise learning, sleep, mental-health and qualified education support, and consider the opportunity cost of the RMB fee.
