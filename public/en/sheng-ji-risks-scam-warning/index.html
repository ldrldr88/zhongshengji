Common Sheng Ji Scam Types

Scam Type 1: No Real Dragon Vein — Just Theatrical Performance

Some providers claim to use a particular 'dragon vein' site but do not provide ownership or lawful-use documents matching the location, rights holder and service date. Check the document issuer, validity and permitted use. 'Dragon vein' and 'earth qi' are traditional Feng Shui descriptions; they do not replace land documents or prove outcomes.

Scam Type 2: Unverifiable Taoist Lineage

If a provider claims an ordination, lineage or professional status, ask for the exact name, date, issuer and original document, then check the issuing body or an independent primary source where possible. An unverifiable claim is a risk signal, but a title alone does not establish service quality.

Scam Type 3: Refusing to Provide Full Ceremony Video

Agree in writing before payment on the steps, what may be recorded, privacy limits and deliverables. Video can show recorded portions but cannot by itself prove identity, land rights, uninterrupted performance or results. Refusal to document the agreed scope is more important than whether every part may be filmed.

5 Core Checks to Verify a Sheng Ji Master

Check 1: Taoist Ordination Record — Specific, Named, and Verifiable

Ask for the claimed ordination date, issuing authority, lineage and title. This site states that Lin Jia-Hua received an ordination in 2007 and uses the Taoist name Lin Yi Chan; treat that as a provider statement until the original record and issuer can be checked. Such a record would support only what it explicitly states, not service outcomes.

Check 2: Real Dragon Vein Land — Documented Ownership

Request ownership, lease or use authorization that matches the actual site, rights holder, validity period and permitted activity. This site calls the location the 'Fu Gui Shuang Quan dragon vein'; that is the provider's traditional Feng Shui name. Site descriptions and video may help identify a place but cannot prove title, authorization or results.

Checks 3–5: Client Videos, Verifiable Public Record & Transparent Fees

Historical client material should be shown only with consent and without exposing birth data, biological samples or other sensitive information. For media and association claims, find the original publisher, date and exact named person. Confirm total price, third-party costs, cancellation, refund and deliverables in writing, and pause if extra payment is demanded on the basis of guaranteed effects.
