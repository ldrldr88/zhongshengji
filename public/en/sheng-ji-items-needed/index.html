Obtain the written materials list first

Common personal items

The provider’s traditional process may request hair, nail clippings, a previously worn garment and birth details. Before paying, obtain an itemised list covering purpose, recipient, retention period, privacy and final handling.

If a biological sample is requested

This website does not provide self-collection instructions. Ask whether a non-invasive alternative is available. If a biological sample is still requested, consult a qualified healthcare professional and confirm courier and customs rules.

Birth data is personal information

Birth date and time, legal name, contact details and video records should be treated as sensitive personal data and shared only with a verified recipient who has a clear need.

Checks before shipping

Verify the recipient and trackable address

Do not send personal items or biological material to an unverified person. Use tracking and keep the written list, shipping record and receipt confirmation.

Follow courier and customs rules accurately

Restrictions vary by location. Ask the courier and customs authority whether the item is allowed and how it must be declared. Do not use an inaccurate description to avoid inspection.

Confirm storage, return and disposal

Obtain written terms covering where items are kept, who may access them, when they are used, whether they are returned and how unused material is destroyed.

Minors and third-party materials

Protect minors

A guardian should assess participation carefully and protect the child’s privacy. Do not use exam, health or family anxiety to create urgency, and do not instruct a minor to collect a biological sample.

Do not submit another person’s items without consent

Do not collect or send another person’s hair, nails, garment, birth data or biological material without their informed consent.

Pause when anything is unclear

If safety, privacy, shipping or purpose is unclear, pause preparation and payment and request complete written instructions via WeChat NSDG521 or the public email address.

Frequently Asked Questions

What materials may be requested?

The traditional process may request hair, nail clippings, a worn garment and birth details, and may request a biological sample. Obtain an itemised written list and purpose statement first.

Does this website instruct self-collection of blood?

No. Ask about a non-invasive alternative and obtain safety advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

What must I check before shipping?

Verify the recipient, address, purpose, retention, privacy, return or disposal terms, and applicable courier and customs rules.
