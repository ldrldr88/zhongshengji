Treat each family member as a separate decision

Obtain informed consent

Adults should decide for themselves after seeing the full service scope and cost. For minors, guardians should weigh privacy, safety and financial priorities; do not collect a child’s personal or biological materials without appropriate consent and safeguards.

Protect private information

Ask how names, birth details, recordings and personal materials will be stored, shared and deleted. Do not use another family member’s details as a marketing example without permission.

Check total family cost

Calculate the whole commitment

Published prices are RMB 72,000 per five-year service and RMB 500,000 per lifetime service. The booking deposit is RMB 10,000 per agreed booking; the balance and any later upgrade difference should be written down before payment.

Check what each person receives

Confirm each practitioner, plot arrangement, schedule, recording, returned items, rescheduling and refund rule. Do not assume one family member’s service covers another.

Do not use repeat purchases as proof

Testimonials have limits

A family’s decision to buy again may reflect belief or preference. It does not establish that a ritual changed inheritance, health, relationships or future wealth.
