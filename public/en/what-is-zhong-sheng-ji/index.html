Zhong Sheng Ji Meaning

A Traditional Practice for a Living Person

Zhong Sheng Ji (種生基), commonly shortened to Sheng Ji, is described in Taoist and Chinese geomancy traditions as a service performed for a person who is still alive. It is sometimes translated as “living tomb” or “life-base Feng Shui”. These English labels are cultural translations, not medical or scientific terms.

How It Differs from Ancestral Tomb Feng Shui

Ancestral tomb, or Yin Zhai, Feng Shui concerns burial sites for deceased ancestors. Zhong Sheng Ji is presented as a separate practice for the living person who commissions the service. The two should not be treated as interchangeable.
